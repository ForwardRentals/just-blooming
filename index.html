What is PEMF

A gentle, pulsing magnetic field, explained simply.

PEMF stands for pulsed electromagnetic field. Inside the mat are coils of wire. When a current runs through them, they create a magnetic field that rises and falls many times each second.

That field passes easily through clothing and the body, the same way the Earth’s own magnetic field does. Physics tells us that a changing magnetic field creates tiny electrical currents in anything that conducts electricity, and the body’s tissues do. This effect, called induction, is the basic idea researchers study when they look at PEMF.

During a session you lie on the mat, fully clothed, and rest. There’s nothing to swallow, apply or do.

Hz

Frequency

How many times per second the field pulses. Many PEMF programs use slow rhythms between 1 and 30 pulses per second.

The Earth’s Schumann resonance: 7.83 Hz

µT

Intensity

How strong the field is, measured in microtesla. Our system works at low intensity, and we always start gently.

Earth’s field: about 25–65 µT · Hospital MRI: 1.5–3 T

∿

Waveform

The shape of each pulse over time. This is where systems differ most, and why we chose a classic analogue one.

Ours: continuous and smoothly varying

Analogue, not digital

A classic analogue system, chosen on purpose.

Digital signals are sampled into steps. Analogue signals are continuous and always subtly changing, the way waves, wind and breath move. Our system is analogue, so its waveform rises and falls smoothly.

Digital

Sampled. Stepped. Discrete values.

Analogue

Continuous. Fluid. Always changing.

A long history

Scientists have studied pulsed electromagnetic fields for more than fifty years. Some medical PEMF devices have been regulated for specific clinical uses since the late 1970s, and research continues today.

Our sessions are not one of those medical uses. We offer PEMF as a wellness experience, and we share this background as information, so you can read further and decide for yourself.

What people describe

Most people say a session simply feels like resting. Some notice warmth, a light tingling, or that they drift toward sleep. Others notice nothing at all.

All of these are normal. Everyone’s experience is different, and we can’t predict or promise how you’ll feel.

We mention the Schumann resonance because it inspires our approach. We don’t claim that PEMF reproduces the Earth’s field, or that any frequency, intensity or waveform has a particular effect on your health.

We respect conventional, naturopathic, integrative and other approaches to health, and your right to choose the practitioners and practices that feel right for you.

If you’re already working with a practitioner, let them know you’re trying PEMF.

When PEMF isn’t suitable

Please don’t book a PEMF session, unless a qualified professional has cleared you, if you:

  • are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • have a pacemaker or another implanted electronic device

If you have a significant health condition, are in active treatment or take medication, check with your practitioner before your first session.

What does a PEMF session feel like?
Most people say it feels like resting. Some notice warmth or tingling. You stay fully clothed, lying down in a quiet room.
Why analogue?
It was a deliberate choice. An analogue system produces a continuous, smoothly changing waveform rather than a stepped digital one. We’re happy to show you how it works.
Will PEMF help my condition?
We can’t make that promise, and we don’t give medical advice. We share information so you can decide for yourself, ideally with a practitioner who knows your health history.
How often do people come?
There’s no set number. Some people come regularly, others now and then. You decide what fits your life.
